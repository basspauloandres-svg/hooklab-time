## Approved direction
HookLab is an evidence-to-creative deduction system, not a hit-prediction engine.

Canonical chain:
`OBSERVATION -> ASSOCIATION -> INTERPRETATION -> HYPOTHESIS -> CONDITIONED DEDUCTION -> MUSICAL REALIZATION -> HUMAN EVALUATION`

## Scientific-layer invariants
- prediction != deduction
- association != causation
- industry claim != scientific evidence
- genre/style is an analytical/aesthetic stratification layer, not a universal success gate
- exposure is a covariate/contextual layer, not a universal explanation
- every deduction retains scope and provenance
- no substantive musical finding may be synthesized to test the architecture
