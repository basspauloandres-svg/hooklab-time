# HookLab Evidence-to-Creative Deduction Framework v1

## Purpose
HookLab is not a hit-prediction system. Its scientific purpose is to transform observed, scoped and corroborated evidence into conditioned creative deductions that can be realized musically and evaluated by humans.

## Epistemic chain
`OBSERVATION -> ASSOCIATION -> INTERPRETATION -> HYPOTHESIS -> CONDITIONED DEDUCTION -> MUSICAL REALIZATION -> HUMAN EVALUATION`

No stage may be silently skipped.

## Evidence classes
1. `OBSERVED_DATA`: reproducible corpus/market/music observation.
2. `SCIENTIFIC_LITERATURE`: peer-reviewed or otherwise methodologically auditable evidence.
3. `INDUSTRY_CLAIM`: professional/network/course/platform claim; hypothesis only until independently corroborated.
4. `INTERPRETATION`: reasoned explanation compatible with evidence; never relabeled as observation.
5. `GENERATIVE_EVIDENCE`: evidence produced by controlled musical realization and evaluation; never backfilled into the original observational corpus.

## Claim levels
- `DESCRIPTIVE`: distribution/pattern within a stated corpus.
- `ASSOCIATIVE`: statistical relationship under stated conditions/controls.
- `EXPERIMENTALLY_SUPPORTED`: supported by an appropriate experimental contrast.

Causal language is prohibited below the experimentally supported level.

## Analytical layers
Observed outcome, exposure/platform mediation, context/metadata, musical data, textual/prosodic data, genre/style, audience/market, statistical relationship, interpretation, rule promotion, creative experiment.

Genre/style is primarily an analytical stratification and aesthetic-context layer. It is not a universal success gate. A genre-specific experiment may still define a genre/style cohort when the research question requires it.

## Deduction contract
A deduction must retain: population scope, evidence id, observed pattern, statistical support, alternative explanations, interpretation, theoretical support, claim level, provenance, bounded musical consequence and human-evaluation target.

A deduction never means `this will be a hit`. It means `given evidence E under scope S, musical consequence C is sufficiently justified to be tested as a creative hypothesis`.

## Industry-claim invariant
Frequency of repetition in professional practice, social media, courses, platform advice or commercial training does not promote a claim to scientific evidence. Such claims enter as hypotheses and require independent empirical corroboration.

## Prototype falsification logic
The framework is directionally supported only if it can:
1. admit a complete associative evidence record without converting it to a prediction;
2. reject a `HIT_PREDICTION` purpose;
3. reject unsupported industry claims;
4. reject causal language without experimental support;
5. preserve genre/style as stratification/context;
6. keep musical realization blocked when the substantive association is not yet observed.

## Current empirical boundary
The observed 300-song frame is a discovery/outcome frame, not yet 300 scientifically promoted musical observations. It can support sampling and metadata work; substantive musical deductions require eligible musical rows with provenance and the relevant analytical gates passed.
