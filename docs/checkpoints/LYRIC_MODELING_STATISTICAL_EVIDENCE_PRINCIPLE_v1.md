# HookLab/TIME-MIE — Lyric Modeling Statistical Evidence Principle v1

Status: CANONICAL INVARIANT
Scope: lyric modeling, Story Brief assisted mode, narrative/text features, conditioned deductions, generative tests.

## Canonical principle

The identification of analytical trends is grounded in empirical evidence obtained from the corpus through pre-specified statistical procedures appropriate to the study design. Evidence is characterized through effect magnitude, uncertainty, robustness and, when applicable, replication.

Scientific literature does not determine in advance the direction or existence of empirical associations. Literature has two delimited functions: (1) support valid conceptual definition and operationalization of admissible constructs before analysis, without prescribing the expected result; and (2) interpret, contextualize and discuss the empirical result after statistical analysis.

## Mandatory inference order

DATA SAYS -> STATISTICS SAY -> THEORY SAYS -> GENERATION TESTS -> PRODUCER DECIDES

This order is invariant.

DATA SAYS does not mean an association found through unrestricted search. A candidate trend must arise from an auditable statistical design.

## Pre-analysis gate

Every lyric/narrative feature must pass:

Feature Admissibility -> Analysis Registration -> Statistical Test

Feature admissibility establishes construct validity and measurement meaning. It does not establish the expected direction, magnitude or existence of an association.

Before inference, declare at minimum:

- RESEARCH_QUESTION
- POPULATION_SCOPE
- OUTCOME
- ADMISSIBLE_FEATURE_IDS
- PRIMARY_TESTS
- COVARIATES
- MULTIPLICITY_FAMILY
- EFFECT_SIZE_CRITERION
- ROBUSTNESS_PLAN
- REPLICATION_REQUIREMENT
- STOP/PROMOTION_RULE

## Statistical evidence requirements

A numerical association is not automatically a trend. Interpretation must consider, as applicable:

- effect size;
- uncertainty intervals;
- sample size and effective information;
- measurement error;
- multiplicity control;
- confounding and alternative explanations;
- sensitivity to analytical decisions;
- robustness;
- replication;
- distinction among exploratory, confirmatory and post hoc analyses;
- distinction among descriptive, associational, experimental and causal claims.

Null, weak, unstable or non-replicated results remain legitimate scientific results.

## Theory boundary

THEORY_SAYS may support, contextualize, contradict or leave unresolved an observed statistical pattern.

Theory/literature MUST NOT:

- create a positive trend absent in the data;
- rescue a null result;
- increase an effect size;
- transform association into causation;
- unlock a generative rule by plausibility alone;
- replace multiplicity, robustness, sensitivity or replication requirements.

## Lyric-modeling promotion rule

A lyric or narrative feature may influence the assisted Story Brief only after the statistical analysis assigns an explicit disposition:

PROMOTE_TO_CONDITIONED_DEDUCTION | HOLD_FOR_REPLICATION | NO_PROMOTION | AUDIT

Only PROMOTE_TO_CONDITIONED_DEDUCTION may enter the evidence-assisted generative layer.

The promoted item remains a conditioned deduction, never a universal songwriting rule and never a success prediction.

## Story Brief architecture

### Mode A — Manual/visible brief

Producer explicitly defines narrative material such as story, characters, relationship, conflict, point of view, scene, emotional trajectory and audiovisual/cinematic reference.

### Mode B — Evidence-assisted brief

The system may construct a hidden/intermediate Story Brief only from features that have passed the admissibility and statistical promotion gates above.

The evidence-assisted layer must operate on abstract features and statistical results. It must not reproduce corpus lyrics or use structural metadata such as SECTION labels as lyrical tokens.

SECTION_FUNCTION is metadata. LYRIC_CONTENT is generated content. These fields must remain separated.

## Experimental comparison

Manual and evidence-assisted Story Brief modes should be compared under matched conditions when feasible:

- same reference audio;
- same compositional intention;
- same target section;
- same generation/evaluation environment.

Producer evaluation should include, where applicable:

- narrative coherence;
- brief adherence;
- textual naturalness;
- lyric-melody integration;
- singability;
- hook strength;
- memorability;
- number of iterations to acceptance;
- time to acceptable candidate.

Human preference is evaluation evidence for the generative realization. It does not retroactively convert a statistical association into scientific proof.

## Fail-closed rules

If a feature lacks admissibility, registered analysis, adequate statistical evidence or the required promotion status, it cannot influence the evidence-assisted Story Brief.

If evidence is null, weak, unstable or unresolved, the correct outputs are HOLD_FOR_REPLICATION, NO_PROMOTION or AUDIT as appropriate.

No literature citation, industry discourse, aesthetic intuition or producer preference may override this gate.

## Canonical lyric-modeling chain

CORPUS
-> ADMISSIBLE LYRIC/NARRATIVE FEATURES
-> REGISTERED STATISTICAL ANALYSIS
-> EFFECT + UNCERTAINTY + MULTIPLICITY + ROBUSTNESS
-> THEORY-SUPPORTED INTERPRETATION
-> CLAIM LEVEL
-> PROMOTION DECISION
-> CONDITIONED DEDUCTION
-> STORY BRIEF
-> LYRIC + PROSODY + MELODY + BEAT RELATION
-> HUMAN EVALUATION
-> PRODUCER DECISION

This chain must remain auditable end to end.
