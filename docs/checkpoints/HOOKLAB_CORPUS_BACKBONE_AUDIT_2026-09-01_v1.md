## A–F separation

- **A — corpus source:** synchronized revision 72 of the CorpusBot/LRCLIB-backed C001-C100 records is the canonical internal source.
- **B — Matrix X and derived matrices:** evidence views, M8/B1, MIDI modality, TIME sample and design matrices. They do not add source lyric observations by existing in multiple files.
- **C — calculated features:** recovered-MIDI status, selected melody tracks, structural audits and historical observations. They remain descriptive until their own calibration/admissibility gates pass.
- **D — prior statistical results:** the 5-reference lyric diagnostic and HOOK-CORPUS results remain frozen; no stable lyric rule was promoted.
- **E — original lyrics/texts:** remain at the authorized provider. This registry stores locators and provenance only, never lyric content.
- **F — provenance:** source/provider declarations, manifests, indexes, hashes or immutable revisions, recovery reports and checkpoint records.

## Deduplication decisions

1. C001-C100 is one case namespace. A case is counted once regardless of its appearances in sheets, MIDI directories or reports.
2. The two 100-song workbooks overlap completely. Revision 72 is canonical and revision 1 is the frozen baseline mirror; together they contribute one C001-C100 population and are not a replication.
3. M8/B1 is a C001-C010 observational subset, not a separate population.
4. The category, relationship, negative-case and M8 draft workbooks are historical derived copies over the same identities; they add neither cases nor independent evidence.
5. MIDI is a linked modality. Its 68 recovered files and 67 melody candidates do not change lyric corpus N.
6. HookLab TIME Sample 1 is a distinct active population and remains separate.
7. External auxiliary datasets and Lakh cannot repair missing HookLab provenance or become the HookLab lyric corpus.
8. Generated candidates are outputs for human evaluation, not empirical source observations.

## Why this is the program backbone

The corpus registry is the evidence backbone, but it is not itself a generator. Its function is to make every later claim traceable:

`authorized source -> admissible feature -> registered analysis -> statistical disposition -> conditioned deduction -> AI coherence/organization -> generation test -> producer decision`

The AI may organize records, map modalities, detect missingness, assemble promoted deductions and audit coherence. It may not create a trend, promote its own rule or convert a descriptive scaffold into scientific evidence.

The source gate is resolved. The composition engine remains `D0_EXPLORATORY` because no narrative feature has completed measurement calibration, analysis registration or statistical testing; evidence-assisted Story Brief remains blocked.
