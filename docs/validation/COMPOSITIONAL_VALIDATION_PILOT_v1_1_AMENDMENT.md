# HookLab Compositional Validation Pilot v1.1 — Amendment

Status: PREREGISTRATION AMENDMENT BEFORE DATA COLLECTION
Date: 2026-09-01
Supersedes only the timing/baseline portions of v1.0; all other scientific boundaries remain.

## Rationale
The original pilot treated `TIME_TO_ACCEPTABLE_HOOK_MIN` as a primary outcome and required a new HUMAN_ONLY timed composition for every brief. Before collecting pilot outcome data, the producer identified that a full unaided composition cycle is long and that repeating six timed HUMAN_ONLY sessions would impose substantial burden while contributing less directly to the immediate question: whether HookLab improves the compositional quality and integration of the hook.

This amendment is prospective: it is registered before observing comparative pilot outcomes.

## Revised primary question
Does HookLab produce or facilitate hooks with higher blind-rated compositional quality and multimodal integration than comparison conditions?

## Primary outcome
`OVERALL_HOOK_EFFECTIVENESS_1_7` — blind evaluator rating.

## Key secondary outcomes
- `TEXT_MELODY_INTEGRATION_1_7`
- `PROSODIC_FIT_1_7`
- `MELODIC_COHERENCE_1_7`
- `HOOK_MEMORABILITY_1_7`
- `SINGABILITY_1_7`
- `ORIGINALITY_PERCEIVED_1_7`
- producer ACCEPT / MODIFY / REJECT / REQUEST_VARIATION
- number and nature of producer revisions to HookLab candidates.

## Timing outcome
`TIME_TO_ACCEPTABLE_HOOK_MIN` is demoted from PRIMARY to EXPLORATORY/PROCESS.

Historical self-reported producer duration may be recorded as contextual evidence only. It must not be analyzed as an experimentally equivalent HUMAN_ONLY comparator because it was not collected under the same brief, clock, stopping rule or observation protocol.

## HUMAN_ONLY condition
A new timed HUMAN_ONLY condition is not required for Pilot v1.1.

If an independently created human hook with adequate provenance and the same brief becomes available prospectively, it may be included as a descriptive/blind comparison under a distinct `HUMAN_REFERENCE` label. It must not be relabeled as randomized HUMAN_ONLY experimental data.

## Pilot v1.1 conditions
Primary comparative pilot:
1. `GENERAL_AI`
2. `HOOKLAB`

For each brief, both conditions receive the same compositional brief and target deliverable. The evaluator must be blind to condition.

## Design
- 6 frozen briefs remain.
- 2 primary conditions × 6 briefs = 12 final candidates.
- Candidate IDs are blinded before evaluation.
- Producer may curate candidates under each condition according to the frozen condition rules.
- Reference audio, when used, must be the same within a brief across conditions where technically applicable and must remain an aesthetic reference rather than success evidence.

## Interpretation boundary
This pilot can estimate comparative hook quality between GENERAL_AI and HOOKLAB under the implemented conditions. It cannot establish that HookLab is faster than unaided human composition, nor that it produces commercially successful songs.

## Promotion rule
No scientific creative rule is promoted from this pilot merely because HookLab wins a preference comparison. Pilot results evaluate the system/product workflow. Any claim about a specific compositional feature still requires the canonical Feature Admissibility → Analysis Registration → Statistical Test chain.

## Canonical reporting separation
`DATA SAYS → STATISTICS SAY → THEORY SAYS → GENERATION TESTS → PRODUCER DECIDES`
