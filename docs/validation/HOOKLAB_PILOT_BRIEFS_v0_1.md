# HookLab Pilot Creative Briefs v0.1

Status: FROZEN FOR PILOT FEASIBILITY
Date: 2026-09-01

These briefs are designed to test process and hook construction rather than genre imitation. Each brief defines an intention and functional constraints while leaving lyrical and melodic expression open.

## B01 — Reencuentro
Create a chorus hook about two people meeting again after a long separation. Emotional direction: restrained hope rather than triumph. Target: 2–4 short lines, singable in a contemporary mid-tempo context.

## B02 — Decisión
Create a chorus hook centered on the moment of choosing to leave a relationship that has become repetitive. Emotional direction: clarity and forward motion. Avoid direct insults or revenge framing.

## B03 — Noche compartida
Create a chorus hook about a fleeting nighttime encounter that feels important without promising permanence. Emotional direction: intimacy and immediacy. Target language should permit rhythmic repetition.

## B04 — Volver a empezar
Create a chorus hook about rebuilding after a personal failure. Emotional direction: vulnerability moving toward agency. Avoid generic motivational slogans where possible.

## B05 — Distancia
Create a chorus hook about maintaining an emotional connection across physical distance. Emotional direction: longing with composure. The hook should support a memorable repeated phrase.

## B06 — Celebración colectiva
Create a chorus hook about a group reaching a meaningful shared moment. Emotional direction: communal energy rather than individual achievement. The text should be suitable for call-and-response or collective singing.

## Common constraints
- Spanish language for pilot comparability.
- Maximum composition time: 20 minutes per condition.
- One final hook candidate frozen per condition.
- The producer may revise freely within the condition's allowed tools.
- No condition may copy protected lyrics or distinctive melodies from a reference.
- HookLab aesthetic references, when used, function only as abstract style/compositional references.
